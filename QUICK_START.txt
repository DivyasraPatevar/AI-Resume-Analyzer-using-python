## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Why is first run slow?**
A: Models downloading (~1.75GB). Subsequent runs: 15-30 sec

**Q: What if my PDF won't extract?**
A: Use a text-based PDF, or convert to .txt format

**Q: Can I disable BERT?**
A: Yes, but it's optional and worth keeping for insights

**Q: Does it store my resume?**
A: No! Everything stays local on your computer

**Q: Will highlighting always work?**
A: Yes, it highlights all 70+ skills + verbs + education keywords

**Q: What's the most useful new feature?**
A: BERT analysis (5 dimension aspect scoring) or keyword highlighting
